# Greenscape Pro AI Agent Strategy

**Decision:** Build the **Quote-to-Win Proposal Copilot** first. Greenscape Pro does not have a lead-generation problem. It has a quote-capacity problem: qualified prospects wait 6–9 days while faster competitors win business.

## 1. Quote-to-Win Proposal Copilot

**Purpose:** Convert Marcus’s messy site-walk notes into a guarded, customer-ready proposal draft within minutes, while keeping him as the final approver.

- Structures notes into scope, quantities, assumptions, exclusions, timeline, and customer-facing copy.
- Matches proposed work to the existing pricing catalog and calculates totals deterministically.
- Flags missing measurements, low-confidence interpretations, HOA issues, and unusual price ranges.
- Preserves every draft and routes the approved result to GoHighLevel (GHL) through a webhook.

**Replaces or unblocks:** Marcus’s manual interpretation, proposal assembly, and re-keying across the pricing sheet, Google Docs, and GHL.

**Estimated ROI:** Compresses 6–9 days to a same-day draft; conservatively recovering one additional average project per month yields **$336,000 annual revenue** and about **$127,680 gross profit** at a 38% project margin. It should also return several founder hours each week.

**Why #1:** It attacks the binding revenue constraint before adding more demand. The pricing catalog and designer are already capable; Marcus’s translation step is the bottleneck.

## 2. Closed-Lost Revenue Reactivator

**Purpose:** Reopen qualified opportunities in GHL with personalized, Marcus-voiced SMS and email sequences.

- Segments 1,400 closed-lost leads by project type, timing, value, and loss reason.
- Produces personal messages from historic notes instead of generic blasts.
- Stops outreach on replies and routes warm responses to Brittany.
- Learns which segments, messages, and offers generate booked calls.

**Replaces or unblocks:** Brittany’s sporadic manual re-engagement and an otherwise idle lead asset.

**Estimated ROI:** A 2% re-close rate equals **28 projects and $784,000 revenue**, or approximately **$297,920 gross profit** at a 38% margin.

**Why #2, not #1:** The upside is large, but lead quality has decayed and more demand worsens the current proposal backlog until Quote-to-Win creates capacity.

## 3. Post-Sign Expeditor

**Purpose:** Move signed projects through deposit, homeowner association (HOA), permit, and design dependencies without Jenna manually chasing every party.

- Tracks each pre-construction dependency and its owner.
- Sends stage-specific reminders and escalates stalled items.
- Produces a daily exception queue instead of a manual status hunt.
- Updates GHL and notifies Jenna only when human intervention is needed.

**Replaces or unblocks:** Repetitive deposit, HOA, permit, and revision follow-up.

**Estimated ROI:** Accelerates **$224,000–$336,000 of delayed revenue** across 8–12 projects at any time, improves crew scheduling, and likely saves Jenna 3–5 hours weekly.

## 4. Build Update Concierge

**Purpose:** Send consistent, branded client progress updates from Jobber milestones and CompanyCam photos.

- Detects meaningful build events and selects relevant project photos.
- Drafts a concise update in Marcus’s voice, with approval for exceptions.
- Sends updates after configurable silence or milestone thresholds.
- Logs delivery, customer replies, and unresolved concerns in GHL.

**Replaces or unblocks:** Ad hoc crew texts, missed Loom updates, and Jenna’s reactive “what is happening?” calls.

**Estimated ROI:** Saves an estimated 2–4 service hours weekly, reduces client anxiety, and protects referral and review volume at low operating cost.

## 5. Lead Gatekeeper

**Purpose:** Qualify inbound leads by SMS before they consume Marcus’s calendar.

- Asks four to five questions covering scope, location, budget, and timing.
- Scores fit against Greenscape Pro’s premium positioning and minimum project profile.
- Routes strong leads to booking, edge cases to Brittany, and poor fits to a courteous decline.
- Writes every answer and disposition back to GHL.

**Replaces or unblocks:** Marcus’s 10–15-minute calls with obviously unqualified leads.

**Estimated ROI:** Avoids roughly **40–90 minutes of founder time per week** based on 4–6 unqualified leads. The larger benefit is protecting same-day response time for good opportunities.

**Why #5:** It is useful but addresses a smaller time leak than quoting, revenue reactivation, pre-construction drag, or customer communication. Over-filtering also risks rejecting unusual high-value work, so rollout should begin with routing—not autonomous rejection.

## Two Explicit Calls

**Why is #1 the #1, and not the founder’s stated #1?** It is the founder’s stated #1, and the evidence supports him. The non-obvious judgment is refusing to prioritize his requested marketing or crew-coaching ideas: healthy 4–4.5× Meta return on ad spend and excess lead volume prove that more demand would amplify the bottleneck. Quote speed is the prerequisite that increases the ROI of reactivation and qualification agents.

**Agent considered but excluded:** A **Marketing Content Agent** was excluded. Social content may strengthen brand recall, but Greenscape Pro already spends $25,000–$30,000 monthly acquiring sufficient demand. Automating content now would optimize a non-constraint while qualified leads continue to expire in the quote queue.
